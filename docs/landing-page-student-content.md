# Landing Page Student Content

This document captures the main student-facing value shown on the landing page.

## Core Promise

Urologics is presented as an FRCS / FEBU urology preparation platform that combines:

- app-based learning
- live online teaching
- tests and mock exams
- AI viva practice
- mentor-led preparation

## What a Student Gets

### 1. App-Based Courses

Students can access structured app-based preparation for:

- FRCS Urology Section 1
- FRCS Urology Section 2
- FEBU Section 1
- FEBU Section 2

Across these courses, the landing page highlights:

- chapter-wise tests
- grand mock tests
- live lectures
- recorded lectures
- practice with mentor
- AI-supported preparation in Section 2 pathways

### 2. AI Viva

The landing page positions AI Viva as a major differentiator.

Students are told they get:

- an AI urology mentor
- different examiner temperaments
- the ability to choose an examiner
- 100+ AI viva scenarios
- personalised feedback scoring after each viva
- progress monitoring during FRCS preparation

### 3. Live Online / Face-to-Face Style Teaching

The live teaching offer includes:

- Section 1 (FRCS / FEBU) live online course
- Section 2 (FRCS / FEBU) live online course
- Section 2 Viva in Dreams
- live one-to-one sessions
- urology masterclasses

The key benefits described on the landing page include:

- structured case-based interactive sessions
- SBA discussion across topics
- Oxford / EAU guideline discussion
- mnemonics, algorithms, and flowcharts
- evidence-based handling of controversial questions
- viva practice with feedback
- time management and communication guidance
- one-to-one personalised mentoring

### 4. Mock and Viva Practice

Students are shown that they can prepare through:

- grand mock tests
- one-to-one grand mock viva exams
- all 8 stations in mock viva format
- individualised sequence and duration for viva practice
- last-minute assessment and feedback support

### 5. Mentor Support

The page presents Dr. Ankit Goel as a major trust signal and learning benefit.

The highlighted student outcomes include:

- trained 200+ candidates worldwide
- evidence-based FRCS urology teaching
- tips, tricks, and mnemonics
- 90%+ pass rates in SBAs and viva

## Overall Student Value

From the landing page alone, the product promise to a student is:

- one place for course learning, tests, mocks, and viva practice
- a mix of self-paced and live preparation
- structured exam-focused teaching
- AI viva exposure for realistic practice
- mentorship-led preparation rather than generic content only
